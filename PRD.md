# AccountPilot AI — Product Requirements Document

## Product Vision

AccountPilot AI is an AI-powered Account-Based Marketing (ABM) strategy and outreach orchestrator. It unifies scattered account data, turns it into evidence-backed account intelligence, and helps revenue teams act with personalized strategy and outreach.

## Target Users

- Enterprise account executives and sales development representatives.
- Account-based marketing teams and revenue operations teams.
- Sales leaders who need pipeline visibility, repeatable account strategy, and trustworthy AI assistance.

## Problem

Customer information is fragmented across CRM records, emails, meeting notes, websites, news, and internal documents. Teams therefore miss decision-makers, overlook buying signals, and send generic outreach. LLM-generated content must not introduce unsupported facts.

## Product Goals

1. Build a unified, continuously updated account profile.
2. Surface stakeholders, pain points, competitors, whitespace opportunities, and buying signals.
3. Recommend explainable next-best actions and account strategy.
4. Generate personalized outreach grounded in approved sources.
5. Make specialized AI-agent activity visible and auditable.

## Core Features

### Account Intelligence

- Ingest mock or connected CRM, email, meeting, website, news, document, and knowledge-base data.
- Present account health, intent, relationship, opportunity, and priority scores.
- Maintain an evidence trail for every material recommendation.

### Research and Stakeholders

- Detect company news, leadership changes, technology stack, hiring trends, and competitive context.
- Map buying committees, influence, decision power, relationship status, and recent engagement.

### Intent and Strategy

- Identify pricing requests, security questions, migration plans, hiring activity, and competitor mentions.
- Produce account-specific pitch angles, solution recommendations, risks, channels, timelines, and next actions.

### Outreach

- Draft email, LinkedIn message, call script, meeting agenda, proposal, and executive brief.
- Support editable tone and regeneration while requiring source-supported claims.

### Operations and Analytics

- Show dashboard pipeline, account health, conversion, stakeholder coverage, and recommendation success metrics.
- Monitor Research, Stakeholder, Intent, Strategy, Outreach, and Verification agents.

## Success Criteria

- Recommendations cite supporting data and state confidence.
- The output is actionable without requiring a salesperson to open multiple systems.
- Different accounts receive meaningfully different strategies and messaging.
- Unsupported claims are blocked or clearly flagged.
