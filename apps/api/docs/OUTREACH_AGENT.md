# AccountPilot AI — Outreach Agent Documentation

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Outreach Agent** (`app.agents.outreach`) generates multi-touch, multi-channel outreach copy tailored to each stakeholder identified in the target account. Crucially, every factual statement generated by this agent must include explicit **citation tags** referencing source documents indexed in the vector store.

---

## 2. Multi-Channel Support

- **Cold & Warm Email Sequences**: Subject lines, opening hooks, body copy, proof points with inline citations, and explicit CTAs.
- **LinkedIn InMail & Connection Messages**: Character-constrained personal touchpoints (< 300 chars for connections).
- **Cold Call Scripts**: Opener, objection handlers, value hooks, and qualification questions.
- **Executive Summaries / One-Pagers**: Bespoke 1-page PDF briefs for C-level executives.

---

## 3. Grounding & Citation Syntax

All generated statements asserting facts, metrics, or case studies must embed citation markers:

```text
Subject: De-risking Acme Corp's European Expansion with Automated Compliance

Hi Jane,

I noticed Acme Corp's recent announcement regarding expansion into the European market [Doc-102:Chunk-1]. As you scale operations across EU jurisdictions, manual SOC2 and ISO audit preparation can slow engineering velocity by up to 4 weeks [Doc-405:Chunk-3].

At [Our Product], we helped Global FinTech Corp automate evidence collection, reducing audit cycles by 85% within 30 days [Doc-882:Chunk-2].

Would you be open to a brief 15-minute conversation next Tuesday to explore how this applies to Acme's roadmap?

Best regards,
Alex Rivera
Account Pilot Executive
```

## 5. System Prompt Guardrails

The Outreach Agent's LLM prompt enforces:
- **Strict Tone Compliance**: Executive, conversational, zero pushy buzzwords.
- **Citation Requirement**: Any ungrounded metric or claim will trigger rejection by the Verification Agent.
- **Variable Injection**: Dynamic insertion of sender name, title, and target company details.
